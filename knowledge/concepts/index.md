# Concepts

- [Execution Traces](execution-traces.md) — A complete record of an AI agent attempting a task, used as the foundation for deriving skills and specifications.
- [Progressive Disclosure](progressive-disclosure.md) — The practice of revealing information to an AI agent in stages rather than dumping everything at once.
- [Session Hygiene](session-hygiene.md) — The practice of intentionally ending AI conversations and starting fresh ones to prevent context drift.
- [Trace-Driven Specification](trace-driven-specification.md) — A specification derived from execution traces rather than written upfront.
- [Agentic Ideation](agentic-ideation.md) — Using an AI agent as a thinking partner during feature exploration.
- [Build Mode Protocol](build-mode-protocol.md) — A structured execution phase where an AI agent implements a pre-agreed plan.
